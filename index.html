About

We don't just manage artists.
We build systems.

GLOBE is a selective artist management company built for the international market. We take on four artists — not four hundred. Every artist in our roster receives a complete career architecture: brand identity, content strategy, booking infrastructure, and long-term positioning engineered for the global stage.

Our network spans 500+ venues across every major territory, 30 brand partners, and 50 organizer relationships. We leverage all of it — exclusively for the artists we believe in without reservation.
